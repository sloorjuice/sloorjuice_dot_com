# Project Details
An easy to setup jukebox service for your home network. Just setup and host this from a device and connect to jukebox.local from any other device to easily add songs to the queue and they'll stream straight to whatever speaker you connect. Very similar to a discord or telegram bot that plays music in audio channels.
